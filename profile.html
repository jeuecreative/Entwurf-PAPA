Marcus Eue · Dipl.-Ing. Metallurgy, RWTH Aachen

I founded Aluminiumfoil-Consult to turn three decades of rolled-aluminium experience into hands-on support for quality, projects and process.

My name is Marcus Eue, and my base sits between Cologne and Aachen. I am an engineer in metallurgy and materials science, trained at RWTH Aachen, with a working life spent inside the rolled-aluminium and foil industry.

The idea behind the practice is simple: help good ideas get built — by covering resource bottlenecks, supporting quality management systems, and bringing an honest outside view to process issues that have stopped moving.

Colleagues and customers have always described the same three things. Goal-oriented. Reliable. Honest. That is the whole brief.
